privacy

We collect nothing about you.

Neither the program nor this site collects, stores or sends anywhere any information about you.

The site

The site has no sign-up, forms, cookies, visitor counters or ads. Fonts and images are hosted on the site itself — no third-party services load when you open a page.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any host, GitHub may log technical request data (such as IP addresses) to protect against attacks, under GitHub’s rules. We have no access to that data.

The release list and the module catalog come from the site itself — from files refreshed on the server once a day. Your browser contacts nothing else.

Download buttons and links in Modules & libraries lead to release files on GitHub; links to VK and Boosty lead to those services. Their rules apply there.

The program

BreezeCAD works fully offline. It doesn’t collect or send your parts and assemblies, device information or usage statistics anywhere. No ads, no analytics.

The program opens and saves only the files you choose: a projects folder (on Android you pick it once in a system dialog) or a single file.

Crash reports

If the program crashes or hangs, it writes a report on the device itself. It isn’t sent anywhere on its own: you can copy it or forward it to the developer — only if you decide to. The Google Play build may additionally send crash data to Google (Android vitals) under Google Play rules — without the contents of your files.

Android permissions

Notifications only — to tell you that the program closed because of an error or isn’t responding. The program doesn’t need internet access.

Questions

Write to the Issues of the BreezeCAD repository or to our VK group.
